About TNHRA

The Tamil Nadu Horse Riders Association was formed on 14 November 2017 by eight founder members and registered on 29 December 2017 (Reg. No. 657/2017, Registrar of Societies, Chennai South).

TNHRA is a Member of the Tamil Nadu Olympic Association. Our objective is to recognise and promote the State's equestrian athletes, working jointly with the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu (SDAT).

TNHRA brings together Member Clubs from Chennai, Kanchipuram, Chengalpattu, Villupuram, Coimbatore, Nilgiris, Erode, Thanjavur, etc. District associations are under formation in line with the National Sports Governance Act, 2025.

Our objectives

  • Recognise and promote the State's equestrian athletes.
  • Promote, conduct and regulate equestrian sport in accordance with the Olympic disciplines.
  • Identify talent in the younger generation in equestrian sports, with SDAT and the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports.
  • Identify and promote sporting talent among differently abled athletes for para-equestrian events, in coordination with SDAT.
  • Conduct certified coaching camps and clinics — stewards, judging, course designing, etc. — and issue certificates.
  • Work jointly with SDAT to promote equestrian sport.
  • Set rules and standards for regional competitions; national and international events follow FEI and EFI rules.
  • Represent the State at the Tamil Nadu Olympic Association and the EFI.

Equestrian Athletes

TNHRA Rider ID

Any equestrian athlete can register for a TNHRA Rider ID card. It is mandatory for all TNHRA competitions and establishes a detailed record of the athlete's achievements.

EFI registration

Equestrian athletes entering EFI events need an EFI rider registration, and horses an EFI horse registration. EFI forms

Entries through TNHRA

Equestrian athletes who are not EFI members may send National Qualifier entries through an EFI-registered club or through TNHRA as the registered State Association.

NSRS registration

Registration on the National Sports Repository System is mandatory for all athletes and coaches. EFI notice

An equestrian athlete may be registered with only one State Association. To move to another State, an NOC from TNHRA is required.

    Honours

    At the Junior National Equestrian Championships 2025 in Bangalore, Tamil Nadu equestrian athletes won 26 medals — 12 gold, 9 silver and 5 bronze — including dressage team medals in all four age groups.
