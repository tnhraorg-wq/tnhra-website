# How to update the TNHRA website (Secretary)

1. Open **tnhra.com/admin** on a computer or phone.
2. Tap **Login with GitHub** and sign in with secretary@tnhra.com.
3. Choose **Website updates**, then what you want to change:
   - Events / competitions
   - News
   - Circulars / notices (attach the PDF)
   - Equestrian athletes
   - Photo gallery
   - Official documents
4. Tap **Add** to add a new entry (new ones go at the bottom of the list), or tap an existing one to edit it.
5. Tap **Publish → Publish now**. The website updates in about 1 minute.

Rules:
- Never put phone numbers, dates of birth, addresses or Aadhaar of athletes on the website.
- Get parents' consent before posting photos of children.
- Use "Equestrian athletes", not "riders", in titles.
- If something goes wrong, tell the President; every change can be undone.
